<b>Messaging how it should be</b>

Whether for your friends, your company or local community, WhiskerChat supports a variety of features to cover all your daily needs.

<b>Easy to use</b>

Simply install, log in and start chatting. The intuitive user interface makes secure communication as easy as possible.

<b>Secure</b>

With olm/megolm end-to-end encryption, encrypted message backups and an automated key trust system, WhiskerChat supports secure communication.

<b>Decentral</b>

Based on the open Matrix protocol, WhiskerChat connects to a decentralized messaging network. Host your own chat server while remaining in contact with everyone.

<b>Feature rich and reliable</b>

From public chats with tens of thousands of participants and polls to custom stickers, spaces, moderation tools, and multi-device support, WhiskerChat delivers a comprehensive communication suite.

<b>Secure video calls</b>

Direct calls and video conferences, fully secured with Matrix RTC-compatible encryption.

<b>Not another walled garden</b>

WhiskerChat is compatible with other Matrix clients like Element, Nheko, Cinny or NeoChat. Learn more at matrix.org.

<b>Made by volunteers</b>

WhiskerChat is an open-source project based on open standards and built for the decentralized Matrix network.
